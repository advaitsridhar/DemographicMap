• Present — a value with a named source and reference year.
  • Not yet available — the figure exists somewhere but this build has not fetched it.
  • Not collected — the country does not gather it at all. France records neither ethnicity nor religion (barred by law); Germany records citizenship and migration background rather than ethnicity; Japan's census asks neither; India collects mother tongue and Scheduled Caste / Tribe status but not ethnicity; the US census may not ask a mandatory religion question.
